# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary: University of Miami students on the Coral Gables campus with a dying phone and no time to sit at an outlet, usually checking this on a phone, often outdoors in bright light. Secondary: people who run campus venues and could host a station. The site is also graded by the BTE 210 professor.

## Product Purpose
Explain PowerUp, a portable charger rental business: how renting works, what it costs, where stations are planned, and why a venue should host one. Success means a student understands it in seconds and a venue operator knows how to get in touch.

## Brand Personality
Quick, upbeat, a little cheeky. Talks like a student who has been at 4% before. Energetic and confident, never corporate.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages, dark "tech" pages with glowing neon, and rows of identical icon cards.

## Design Principles
- Every feature must serve the business model and be defensible in the class presentation.
- Speak to both sides of the business: renters and venues.
- Be honest about what is planned or placeholder. Never imply live stations or a university partnership.
- Stay specific to UMiami rather than writing copy that could fit any campus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, and reduced-motion support. No information carried by color alone.

Note: written from the project brief and Jack's direction ("make it look good, visually appealing and harmonic"), not from a formal design interview.
