Quick answers, before your battery goes.

Renting

Do I need the app?

Yes. The app finds stations, shows the price before you pay, and tracks your rental live. You'll scan the QR code on a station with it to release a charger. App access is free for your first month, then $10 a month.

Will it work with my phone?

Each charger has built-in cables, so there's nothing else to carry.

Who can rent?

Anyone with the app: students, campus visitors, and customers at participating venues. We're starting on the University of Miami's Coral Gables campus.

What does it cost?

$2 an hour, for up to 24 hours, plus app access at $10 a month after a free first month. Students get their first 30 minutes free on their first rental. See full pricing.

Returning

Do I have to return it to the same station?

No. Any open slot at any PowerUp station works. That's the point.

Will I get a reminder?

Yes. The app sends an automatic reminder before your 24 hours are up, so you can return it before late fees start.

How do I know the return worked?

The charger clicks in and the app confirms that your rental has ended.

What if a station is full?

The app shows open slots at nearby stations so you can head to the closest one. We also move chargers between stations to keep slots open.

What if I keep it too long or lose it?

After 24 hours, a late fee of $5 an hour applies. Late fees stop at $240 after 48 more hours. At that point the charger counts as not returned. See pricing.

Help and partners

Something went wrong with my rental. Who do I contact?

Reach our support team in the app or through the contact page. We handle payment, charging and return problems.

Where are the stations?

See our planned locations on the Coral Gables campus.

Is PowerUp part of the University of Miami?

No. PowerUp is an independent student startup concept and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the university.

Can my venue or student organization partner with PowerUp?

Yes. Venues can host a station and earn a share of every rental, and student organizations can help us launch. Start on the partners page.
