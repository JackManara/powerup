Charging with PowerUp

A campus day outlasts a phone battery.

Your phone is your ID, your notes, your ride and your way home. Here's how one long Tuesday can go.

  1. 8:00 am Leave the dorm at 100%.
  2. 12:00 pm Notes, maps and a lot of group chats later: 47%.
  3. 2:00 pm Down to 19% at the library. Rent a charger on the way in.
  4. 4:30 pm Back to 88%. Drop the charger at the Wellness Center.
  5. 10:00 pm Still at 38% for the walk home. Without PowerUp, the phone died around 4.

Thirty seconds, then you're moving again.

  1. Find a station

    Stations sit where your day already takes you: the library, the student center, your classroom building, the gym.

  2. Scan and go

    Scan the station's QR code with the PowerUp app and a charger pops out, cables built in.

  3. Drop it anywhere

    Return it to any PowerUp station, not the one you took it from. Rental ends the moment it clicks in.
