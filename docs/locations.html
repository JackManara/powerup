Where your day already goes.

We're launching on the Coral Gables campus with seven planned stations, each picked for how students actually move around. Select a stop to see why it made the list.

  • Long study sessions drain phones, and nobody wants to give up a good seat to hunt for an outlet.

  • The crossroads of campus. Students pass through between classes, so it is the easiest place to grab a charger or drop one off.

  • Back-to-back classes leave no time to sit by an outlet. A station on the way into class solves it in seconds.

  • A classroom building on the far side of campus from the lake, so students there don't have to cross campus to return a charger.

  • Where the day starts and ends. Residents can return a charger on the way home instead of walking back across campus.

  • Gives south campus residents a station close to home, so the network reaches both ends of a typical day.

  • Music, workout tracking and the walk home all run on a phone that has already been through a full day of class.
