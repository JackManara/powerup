Rent it. Charge it. Drop it anywhere.

No cords to remember and no outlet to camp next to. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

  1. Find a station

    Open the PowerUp app to see stations near you and which ones have chargers ready. Every station is somewhere you'd already walk past.

  2. Scan to rent

    Scan the QR code on the station and see the price before you confirm payment in the app. A fully charged unit releases from its slot.

  3. Charge on the move

    Each charger has built-in cables, so there's nothing else to bring. Track your rental live in the app, and you'll get a reminder before your time runs out.

  4. Return anywhere

    Slide it into an open slot at any PowerUp station. Your rental ends as soon as the station detects it, and the app confirms.
