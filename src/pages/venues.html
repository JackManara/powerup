What your venue gets

  • A share of every rental. Each charger rented from your station earns your venue a portion of the revenue.
  • A reason to stay. Someone with a charger keeps studying, eating or hanging out instead of heading home to plug in.
  • A reason to come back. Every rental ends with a return, and that return can bring someone through your doors.
  • A spot on the map. Your venue shows up in the PowerUp app for everyone looking for a charge nearby.
  • Your brand on the station. Partners can co-brand a station and its chargers to promote discounts at their store.

What we take care of

  • Installation and promotion. We set up the station, its QR code, and posters that point people to it.
  • Payments and rentals. Everything runs through the PowerUp app, so your staff never takes a payment.
  • Keeping it stocked. We move chargers between stations so yours always has some ready, and we replace broken ones.
  • Support. Payment, charging and return questions come to us, not to your front desk.

How hosting works

  1. Pick an annual or semi-annual hosting plan, plus a one-time installation fee.
  2. Give us a small spot where people already pass, near one standard power outlet.
  3. Earn a share of the revenue from every rental at your station.

Student organizations: help launch PowerUp.
